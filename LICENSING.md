# Licensing

The paper this design comes from (Gallup et al. 2020, *HardwareX* 8, e00135)
releases it under the GNU General Public License v3.0 and the CERN Open Hardware
Licence v1.2, in its specifications table. Its design-files table lists the Gerbers,
`Brackets.scad`, the corner, edge and plate meshes, and the KiCad project as GPL-3.0;
the legs and top are named elsewhere in the paper. This repository applies both
licences to all of it:

| Files | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| `mech/` (the bracket source and the printed parts), `elec/` (the KiCad project, netlist and Gerbers), `README.md`, `.gitattributes` | GPL-3.0 and CERN OHL v1.2 |

The texts are in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES): [`GPL-3.0.txt`](LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt)
and [`CERN-OHL-1.2.txt`](LICENSES/CERN-OHL-1.2.txt). The root [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)
is the same GPL-3.0 text, because GitHub reads only that file.

Two of the printed parts, the legs and the top, were also released alongside the
swab in [ngallup4/Nasal-Swab-Files](https://github.com/ngallup4/Nasal-Swab-Files),
whose licence file carries the notice "Copyright (c) 2020 ngallup4".
